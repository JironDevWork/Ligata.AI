## Chat on this website

You can reach us through a chat window on our website. The chat is provided by our own website server (software: Ligata AI) and sets no cookies<!-- if captcha --> (for Google reCAPTCHA see below)<!-- endif -->. Which data we process depends on how you use the chat.

<!-- if ai -->
### AI assistant

The assistant in the chat is an AI system. Its answers are generated automatically and may be wrong or incomplete; please check important information. Please do not enter sensitive data in the chat, for example health data, passwords or bank and credit card details.

**Data we process**

- your messages to the assistant and the conversation so far (it is sent again with every question so the assistant knows the context)
<!-- if images -->
- images, for example screenshots, that you attach to a message
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if pdfs -->
- PDF files that you attach; we process the text read from them and do not store the file
<!-- endif -->
<!-- if pagecontext -->
- the title and path of the page on which you use the chat, and the current date
<!-- endif -->
- your IP address, which is technically needed to connect to our server. We do not store it. For limits against abuse (for example the number of questions per period) we derive a pseudonymous identifier from it with a secret key, which we keep in memory only.

**Purpose and legal basis**

<!-- if consent -->
We process this data to answer your questions. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which we ask for in the chat before your first question<!-- if cookiebot --> (through the "{{cookiebotCategory}}" category in our cookie settings)<!-- endif -->. Without your consent we send no data to the AI service.<!-- if team --> You can still reach us without the AI assistant, as described below.<!-- endif -->

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: in the chat under "Conversations", then "Withdraw consent"<!-- if cookiebot -->, or in the cookie settings<!-- endif -->. Processing before the withdrawal remains unaffected.

The limits against abuse are based on our legitimate interest in running the chat securely and economically (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Storage period**

We do not store conversations with the AI assistant on our server. For statistics we only keep anonymous daily counts, for example the number of questions and the answer time, without any link to people or content. Your browser keeps the conversation (see "Storage in your browser").

It contains neither your IP address nor any conversation content. The legal basis is our obligation to demonstrate consent (Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR). ### Chat with our team

When you write to our team through the chat, we process your name and email address if you provide them, your messages, the title and path of the page, the language of the chat and the times of the messages.<!-- if ai --> If you start the conversation from the AI assistant, we include the conversation with the assistant so our team knows the context.<!-- endif --> To protect against abuse we store a pseudonymous identifier with each conversation, derived from your IP address with a secret key; we do not store the IP address itself.

Conversations are stored in the database of our website server. They are visible to the responsible staff in our administration area; we notify our team about new requests and messages by email [add your email provider]. We can answer you in the chat and, if you provide an email address, by email.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request concerns a contract or steps before entering into one, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Conversations without a new message end after {{inactivityDays}} days. We delete ended conversations automatically {{retentionDays}} days after they end. Notifications in our outgoing email queue are deleted 7 days after sending. On request we delete your conversation earlier.

### Storage in your browser

The chat sets no cookies. So that your conversations remain when you move between pages, it stores data in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device; only content you send in the chat is transmitted to us.

- **`ligata-ai:v2:` followed by our domain name:** your conversations (texts; of attachments only the file names), access keys to conversations with our team and whether the chat window is open. Only created once you use the chat; conversations are removed after {{storageDays}} days without activity.
<!-- if consent -->
- **`ligata-ai:consent:` followed by our domain name:** your consent to the AI assistant (random identifier, version, time). Kept for {{consentDays}} days or until you withdraw it.
<!-- endif -->

This storage is strictly necessary for the chat you expressly want to use (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete the data at any time, in the chat with "Remove from this device" or in your browser settings.
